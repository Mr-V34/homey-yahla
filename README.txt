YAHLA keeps watch over your home network and is built for UniFi. It reads the logs UniFi Activity Logging (syslog) sends to Homey and tells you the moment a device you have not approved joins: a new phone, an unknown laptop on the guest Wi-Fi, or something plugged into a free network port. Approve the devices you know once, and from then on only strangers raise an alert. YAHLA is developed and tested with UniFi; other routers that can send syslog, such as ASUS and OpenWrt, should work too.

YAHLA works on your Homey alone, with no cloud and no extra permissions. Alerts arrive in the timeline and in your Flows, so you decide whether a stranger turns on the lights, sends a push message or sounds the alarm. YAHLA also tells you when the logs stop coming, so silence is never mistaken for safety.
