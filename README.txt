YAHLA keeps watch over your home network. It reads the logs your UniFi network already sends (the Activity Logging export in UniFi Network), and tells you the moment a device you have not approved joins your network: a new phone, an unknown laptop on the guest Wi-Fi, or something plugged into a free network port. Approve the devices you know once, and from then on only strangers raise an alert.

YAHLA works on your Homey alone, with no cloud and no extra permissions. Your approvals are protected by a PIN, and YAHLA tells you when something is wrong with itself: when the logs stop, when someone floods it with fake events, or when its settings are changed behind its back. Alerts arrive in the timeline and in your Flows, so you decide whether a stranger turns on the lights, sends a push message or sounds the alarm.
